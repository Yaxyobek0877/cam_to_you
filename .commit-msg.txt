ISSUE 2 — Stream stopped after ~5 seconds
Root cause: YouTube rejects streams that don't match their spec.
Several issues with our pipeline:

  Video:
  - No -profile:v specified (NVENC default works but explicit is safer)
  - Adaptive keyframes possible (YouTube REQUIRES fixed 2-sec)
  - B-frames present (YouTube prefers none for live)

  Audio:
  - Output was MONO (Hikvision sends mono PCM mu-law, our AAC kept mono)
  - YouTube REQUIRES STEREO audio for live streams
  - This is why stream connected but YouTube dropped after 5s

FIXES in builder.go:
- NVENC: +"-profile:v", "high", +"-keyint_min", GOP, +"-bf", "0"
- x264:  +"-profile:v", "high", +"-keyint_min", GOP, +"-sc_threshold", "0"
- Audio: +"-ac", "2" — forces mono→stereo upmix for YouTube compatibility

VERIFIED with real camera:
- Pipeline produces 4 MB FLV in 8 sec
- Audio stream now reports: "stereo, 128 kb/s" (was mono)
- 2.2x realtime encoding speed (smooth)
